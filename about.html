About GlideBlinds

Your trusted partner for premium custom blinds and shades across Canada. Discover our story, values, and commitment to transforming homes with quality window coverings.

Our Story

Founded with a passion for transforming Canadian homes, GlideBlinds has been serving the Ottawa and surrounding Area with premium window coverings for years. What started as a small local business has grown into a trusted name in custom blinds and shades.

We believe that every window tells a story, and our mission is to help you tell yours beautifully. From the first consultation to the final installation, we're committed to exceeding your expectations with quality products and exceptional service.

As a proudly Canadian company, we understand the unique needs of Canadian homes - from energy efficiency during harsh winters to light control during bright summer days. We're proud to serve the Ottawa region and surrounding communities.

Our Mission

To provide every Canadian home with beautiful, functional, and affordable window coverings that enhance comfort, privacy, and style while supporting local craftsmanship and sustainable practices.

Our Values

Quality: We use only premium materials and proven manufacturing techniques.

Service: Your satisfaction is our top priority from consultation to installation.

Innovation: We stay current with the latest designs and technology.

Community: Supporting Canadian manufacturing and local jobs.

What We Do

In-Home Consultations

Custom Design

From fabric selection to hardware finishes, we customize every detail to match your home's unique aesthetic and functional requirements.

Professional Installation

Our certified installers ensure perfect fit and finish with every installation, backed by our satisfaction guarantee.

Ongoing Support

We provide maintenance tips, warranty support, and are always available for questions or additional projects.
