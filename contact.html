Frequently Asked Questions

How long does installation take?
Most residential installations take 2-4 hours on an average depending on the number of windows. We'll provide a time estimate during your consultation.
Do you offer free consultations?
Yes! We provide free in-home consultations where we measure your windows and discuss your needs and preferences.
What's your warranty policy?
We offer a comprehensive warranty on all our products and installation work. Warranty terms vary by product type.
Can I get fabric samples?
Absolutely! We provide free fabric samples so you can see colors and textures in your home's lighting before making a decision.
How much do custom blinds cost?
Pricing varies based on size, material, and features. Contact us for a free quote tailored to your specific needs and budget.
Do you service commercial properties?
Yes, we provide window covering solutions for offices, retail spaces, and other commercial properties throughout the GTA.
